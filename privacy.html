Privacy Policy

How Silent Library handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Silent Library respects the privacy of our visitors and members.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form, we may collect your name, email address, subject and message. We only collect information that you choose to provide.

How we use information

Your information is used to reply to enquiries and provide library assistance. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies

This simple website does not use advertising cookies. Basic technical information may be recorded for security and performance.

Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information. Please do not send passwords or payment details through the form.

Your choices
